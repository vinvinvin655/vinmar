Structural Engineering Basics

Course Code: SE101
Duration: 8 weeks

About This Course

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to structural engineering principles. You'll learn about the fundamental concepts of structural analysis, design methodologies, and the behavior of different structural elements under various loading conditions.

By the end of this course, you'll be able to analyze simple structures, understand the principles of structural design, and apply basic engineering concepts to solve structural problems.

Course Lessons

Lesson 1: Introduction to Structural Engineering
Pending

An overview of structural engineering, its history, and its importance in civil engineering. Learn about the basic principles and concepts that form the foundation of structural analysis and design.

Lesson 2: Forces and Equilibrium
Pending

Understanding the fundamental concepts of forces, moments, and equilibrium. Learn how to analyze and calculate forces acting on structures and how to determine if a structure is in equilibrium.

Quiz 1: Forces and Equilibrium Quiz
Pending

Test your understanding of forces, moments, and equilibrium concepts covered in Lessons 1 and 2.

Lesson 3: Structural Analysis Methods
Locked

Explore different methods used in structural analysis, including the method of joints, method of sections, and virtual work method. Learn how to apply these methods to analyze trusses and beams.

Lesson 4: Beam Analysis
Locked

Learn about different types of beams, their behavior under various loading conditions, and how to analyze them. Understand concepts like shear force, bending moment, and deflection.

Quiz 2: Structural Analysis and Beams Quiz
Locked

Test your understanding of structural analysis methods and beam analysis covered in Lessons 3 and 4.
